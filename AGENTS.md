# AGENTS.md

homebrew-tap is the shared Homebrew tap for scoutbin tools.

## Rules

- Formulae are generated and pushed by the spine release workflow. Do not hand-edit generated formulae.
- The scoutbin org is on GitHub's free plan. Do not rely on paid features.
- Make minimal changes.
- Own the files you touch. Fix nearby defects and unclear wording you notice, keep the change focused on the task, and call out larger improvements instead of silently widening scope.
- Confirm before any action with side effects, such as pushing, tagging, publishing or editing repo settings.
- Keep commit messages short and imperative. No ticket prefix, no AI or co-author trailers.
